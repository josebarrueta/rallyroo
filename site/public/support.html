Rallyroo Support

For beta access, account help, privacy requests, or a problem with your family schedule, email us.

Email support@rallyroo.dev

When contacting support

Describe what you expected and what happened. You may include the approximate time of the problem and your app version.

Please do not send: passwords, verification codes, session tokens, private calendar subscription URLs, OAuth client secrets, or screenshots containing those values.

Account and privacy requests

You can sign out or initiate permanent account deletion from the Account section in Rallyroo Settings. For access, correction, child-information, or deletion questions, email support with the subject “Privacy request.” We may need to verify that you control the relevant account.

Emergency use

Rallyroo is a coordination tool, not an emergency or safety service. Contact local emergency services or the appropriate caregiver directly for urgent needs.
