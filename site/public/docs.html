Get started

Rallyroo is currently an invitation-only TestFlight beta. Ask for access, then sign in with Apple or Google. A parent can create a Family and invite another parent or a kid. Use the Schedule tab for the day's plans and Settings to manage optional tools.

Schedule: activities and responsibilities

Add an Event for something that occupies time, with its participants, driver, and location. Use a Reminder for a task with a due time; Reminders do not block Schedule time. Rallyroo can show overlapping commitments and recurring occurrences without changing the original Event.

Parents can connect supported iCalendar subscriptions in Settings. Imported Events are read-only; Personal calendars are visible only to the parent who connected them. Schedule drafts from text, voice, or image text are proposals for a parent to review before saving.

Day Brief

A parent can enable a private morning brief and choose weekday, weekend, and holiday times in Settings. The brief draws on that Member's visible plans. If there is a verified Travel plan leave time, it can arrive earlier; an unavailable route never becomes a guessed leave time.

Day Brief notifications use normal priority and respect your device's Focus settings. Delivery behavior may depend on the API and TestFlight version you're using.

Shopping and Pantry

Parents organize Pantry items and store-specific Shopping routines. Family requests and Stock observations provide evidence for a trip plan; a parent reviews and finalizes the decisions, then records outcomes. A Purchase is history, not proof of what is currently in Stock.

Commuter

When enabled by a parent, Commuter can show Caltrain trains by direction and help configure commute alerts. Live train and service data depends on the external provider and may be delayed or unavailable. Check the provider's status before relying on it.
