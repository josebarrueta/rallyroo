Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026

This policy explains how Jose Barrueta, operating Rallyroo (“Rallyroo,” “we,” or “us”), handles information when you use the Rallyroo iOS application, website, and related services.

In short: Rallyroo uses family schedule information to provide the service. We do not sell personal information, serve behavioral advertising, or track people across other companies’ apps and websites.

Information we process

  • Account information: identity-provider user identifiers, name, and email address supplied through Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Family information: names, family roles, optional grade or birth-year descriptions, and display colors.
  • Schedule information: event names, dates, times, participants, drivers, locations, recurrence, status, and conflict information.
  • Invitations: recipient email addresses, invited roles, expiration information, and evidence of guardian authorization for child invitations.
  • Connected calendars: encrypted subscription URLs, provider metadata, imported event details, and synchronization status. Imports are one-way and read-only.
  • Device and operational information: push notification tokens, request identifiers, limited security logs, IP-derived network information, and diagnostic events needed to operate and protect the service.
  • AI-assisted schedule drafts: voice transcripts, text recognized on-device from images, and text you enter when you ask Rallyroo to propose events or reminders. Rallyroo does not upload the selected image or retain raw microphone audio.
  • Support communications: information you choose to send when requesting help.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide and synchronize family schedules, deliver invitations and notifications, search for addresses you request, import connected calendars, detect conflicts, create review-only schedule drafts you explicitly approve, provide support, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and comply with law. We do not use connected-calendar data or family content to train advertising models.

Children and family invitations

Rallyroo is designed for families but is not intended for children to create independent families. A person creating a family without an invitation must be an adult. A child may join only after an adult parent or legal guardian sends a child invitation and confirms authorization for Rallyroo to process the child’s account and family schedule information. Parents and guardians control the family information they provide and may contact us to review or delete a child’s information.

If you believe a child joined without appropriate parent or guardian authorization, contact support@rallyroo.dev so we can investigate and remove the information.

Providers

Rallyroo uses providers that process limited information on our behalf:

  • Stytch for authentication and session management
  • Apple and Google as sign-in providers
  • Google Places for address searches initiated by users
  • Apple Speech Recognition for voice transcription when you choose the Speak feature; availability and on-device processing depend on the device and language
  • Apple Push Notification service for notifications
  • Resend for family invitation and operational email delivery
  • Cloudflare for DNS, encrypted traffic delivery, and security
  • Infrastructure and backup providers for encrypted hosting and recovery

Image text recognition runs on the device. Schedule-draft extraction runs through a Rallyroo-operated local language model; the extracted text and limited family-member names and identifiers are used for that request and are not retained by the model service or used for training.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments. Rallyroo does not give them family data for behavioral advertising.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and family information while needed to provide Rallyroo. You can initiate permanent account deletion inside Settings. If another authenticated account remains, shared family records stay available to that family and references to your deleted profile are removed. If yours is the final authenticated account, Rallyroo deletes the family dataset, including events, invitations, device tokens, and connected calendars.

Security logs are retained only for operationally necessary periods. Deleted records may remain in encrypted disaster-recovery backups until those backups expire through their scheduled lifecycle, targeted at no more than 30 days, and are not restored except for disaster recovery.

Security

We use encrypted network connections, provider-managed authentication, narrowly scoped secrets, encrypted calendar subscription URLs, access controls, and monitored infrastructure. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may update family information in the app, disconnect calendar sources, disable optional notifications, sign out, or delete your account. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by contacting support. California residents may have additional rights under applicable law; Rallyroo does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

International processing

Rallyroo is operated from California, United States. Information may be processed in the United States and in locations where our providers operate.

Changes

We may update this policy as Rallyroo changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@rallyroo.dev.
